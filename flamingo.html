About the app

A door to imagination and knowledge for every child.

Flamingo gives children a rich library that feeds their senses, with printed, audio and animated books gathered in one integrated app.

The content is designed to build the skills a child needs in the 21st century, within a safe and enjoyable reading experience that stays true to values and ethics.

What is inside

Paper books

Order printed stories from the publisher's catalog straight from the app.

E-books

Digital editions that children can read on any phone or tablet.

Animated books

Stories that move, to hold attention and spark imagination.

Audio books

Narrated stories for listening time, bedtime and the car.

Parents' blog

Articles on reading habits, behavior and child development.

Easy subscription & ordering

A simple flow to subscribe, order and keep the library growing.
